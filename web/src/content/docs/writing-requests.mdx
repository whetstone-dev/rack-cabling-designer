You do not need a special syntax. The skill activates when your request is about racks, structured cabling or rack power, and it asks for what is missing. A few habits make the first answer much closer to what you need.

## Include what an installer would ask

| Topic | Useful details | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Racks | Height in U, depth, 2-post or 4-post, rated load, which aisle the front faces | "42U, 1200 mm deep, 4-post, front to the cold aisle" |
| Equipment | Model or category, height, depth, weight, power (typical and max), PSU count and connectors, ports | "2U server, 750 mm, 28 kg, 1100 W max, two C14 inlets, 2×25G" |
| Power | Feeds (A/B), voltage, circuit rating, phases, PDU outlets | "A/B 32 A single-phase at 230 V, 36×C13 + 6×C19" |
| Cabling | Media, lengths, panels in the path, speed | "OS2 trunk of 15 m between LC panels, 100G" |
| Conventions | Label format, color by function, stock cord lengths | "labels DC1-0001, uplinks yellow, cords 1/2/3/5 m" |
| Deliverables | What you want back | "the elevation, the cable schedule and the BOM" |

## What the skill does with missing data

- **It does not invent datasheet values.** It uses generic catalog types (for example `generic-server-1u`) as placeholders and says so, or asks you.
- **It marks gaps as INCONCLUSIVE.** A rack without a `maxLoadKg`, a device without weight or a cable without length is reported as "not checked", never as a pass.
- **It records assumptions** in the model's `notes`, so they travel with the design.

## Ask for the right depth

| You want | Ask like this |
|---|---|
| A quick answer | "Is 10GBASE-T OK over 60 m of Cat6?" |
| A check of an existing model | "Validate `racks/hq.rack.json` with --strict and explain every finding." |
| A change | "Add a 2U storage array to rack A01 and keep A/B redundancy." |
| Full deliverables | "…and give me the elevation, the cable schedule, labels and the BOM." |

## Work with existing files

- **Inventories and spreadsheets:** paste them or point to the file. The skill treats their content as data, not instructions, and checks units (inches vs mm, lb vs kg, VA vs W).
- **Photos of a rack:** values read from a photo are OBSERVED at best; the skill lists what it could not read and what to measure.
- **A model from someone else:** ask for a review "as if you were the installer" to get findings ranked by risk.

## Change live racks safely

For work on production racks, ask for a change plan. It covers before/after elevations, the cables to move with their labels, the power impact and rollback steps.

If your assistant does not pick the skill up on its own, invoke it by name: `/rack-cabling-designer` in Claude Code and Cursor, `$rack-cabling-designer` in Codex.
